# Monetization Policy: Basketball Empire

Every feature that touches money, ads or randomized rewards is checked against this list (in the spirit
of Claude Code Game Studios' live-ops ethics guidance).

1. **Prices are exact.** Show the store's localized price for real-money items, with no fake "was" prices
   and no currency tricks. Confirmation dialogs state the item, its contents and the price.
2. **Odds are fully disclosed** before every pull, along with pity rules and counters. Odds never change
   silently, and pity never gets worse.
3. **No pressure tactics.** No fake countdowns, no "only X left", no streak punishment (a missed login day
   simply restarts the ladder), no guilt messages, and no surprise purchase prompts in the middle of play.
4. **Everything is earnable.** Gems, tickets, gear and athletes all come from play. Purchases speed things
   up or are cosmetic. Nothing in progression requires paying.
5. **Ads are optional and rare.** Rewarded ads are always opt-in and capped per day. Interstitials only
   play at natural breaks (after a championship or world intro), not in a session's first 20 minutes, at
   most once every 15 minutes, and never for VIP players.
6. **Honest integration.** Without a real `window.BEPlatform` the game stays in clearly labeled
   development mode: test purchases never charge, and ad placeholders say they are placeholders.
7. **No targeting of vulnerable players.** No spend-based difficulty, no selling to players who appear to
   be minors, and no offers triggered by losses.
