# Legend of Slime: character and collection systems (public-source reference)

*Compiled 2026-11-10 from public sources (bottom). Paraphrased; nothing from the game's files.
**unknown** marks what could not be confirmed.*

## The cast on screen
- **One hero (the slime)** plus **up to 5 companions** fighting beside it. That is the "squad": the
  hero is who you are; companions are the collectible teammates. Enemies are humans in waves.
- Heroes are swapped from a Change Character screen (bottom slime icon). Each hero has its own level,
  unique skill (e.g. the starter's at level 35) and owned effects at 10/25/50/75/100/star.

## Collection tracks

| Track | Obtain | Equip | Grow | Passive even if unequipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heroes (slimes) | Login (7-day), gems (9900), paid, 2026 Slime Summon with pity | 1 active | EXP shards; promotion at 100 with Tears | Owned effects per milestone level |
| Gear | Gem summons; commons drop from mobs | Slots unlock with level (2 at start) | Duplicates enhance; at 100, 5 dupes → next item | Owned effect (ATK/HP) |
| Skills | Gem summons | Slots unlock by stage; auto-cast | Duplicates fuse | Owned ATK effect |
| Companions | Gem summons | Up to 5 | Duplicates fuse | Owned effect |
| Relics | Forks from Village Raid | Equipped set | Upgrade with forks; promotion with Tears | Owned ATK/HP |
| Traits | Trait points from Dwarven King | 5 lines | Reroll, lock, save/load | Set synergies |
| Toys, Talismans, Magic Items (later additions) | Summons | **unknown** | **unknown** | **unknown** |

## Rarity and odds
- Rarity ladder for gear/skills/companions: common → uncommon → rare → epic → legendary → mythic
  (mythic skills are named on the wiki). Summon odds depend on **banner level 1–10**, viewable in
  game; legendary appears from banner level 6.
- Hero grades go up to **SS**. 2026 hero summon: SS pity at 100, S+ every 10, 3-slot wish list.

## Why every pull feels good
1. Duplicates enhance; at max level they convert upward. Nothing is ever a dead pull.
2. Owned effects: the collection itself is a stat sheet, shown in a collection book with set bonuses.
3. Banner level rises with pulls, so pulling now makes future pulls better.
4. Free pulls every day (3 ads per banner), with ad pull size that grows.

## What this means for a 5-athlete squad
- The hero/companions split maps to **starters**: the five on court are the squad; the bench and
  collection feed it. Our athletes should get **owned effects** (collection power that counts even on
  the bench) and **milestone unlocks** at levels 10/25/50/75/100, so levelling anyone matters.
- Gear = **accessories** with equip + owned effects and slots that unlock by stage (sneakers →
  jersey → headband → wristband → compression, matching our 5 types).
- Skills = **plays/abilities**: slots unlock by world; duplicates level them; auto-cast toggle.
- Companions = **coaches or mascots** as a later third track, if needed.
- Relics/traits/research = **facilities, tactics and training** we already have; give them owned
  effects and a visible upgrade ladder rather than inventing new currencies.
