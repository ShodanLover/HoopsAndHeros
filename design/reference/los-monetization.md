# Legend of Slime: monetization (public-source reference)

*Compiled 2026-11-10 from public reporting and store pages (bottom). Figures are analyst estimates
unless stated. Every idea here is filtered through `design/monetization-policy.md` before it enters
Basketball Empire: real prices shown exactly, full odds, no fake timers, nothing pay-gated, ads
optional and rare.*

## Scale (as reported)
- Released globally July 2022. By May 2023: about 20M downloads and over $40M lifetime IAP
  (mobilegamer.biz citing data.ai/Appmagic). Analyst estimate: ads ≈ 60% of revenue, IAP < 40%.
- App Store (2026 snapshot): 4.7 stars from 42K ratings, 661 MB, 9+, "Loot Boxes" and
  "Advertising" disclosed, 10 languages.

## Ad design (the "admon masterclass")
- Roughly 30 placements, 12–20 impressions per player per day (3–4× genre norms). A Korean industry
  review counted 14–15 ad slots and estimated a 70–75% voluntary view rate.
- Every ad is **opt-in and pays**: 20-minute buffs that grow stronger with consecutive use; free
  summons whose size grows with use; roulette spins; dungeon re-entries; mine tools; research skips;
  AFK ×2; flying chests over the battle; "out of currency" offers of a few more attempts.
- Remove Ads ($29.99) is rated the best purchase by players, which shows how much the loop leans on
  ads for free players.

## IAP catalogue (US App Store, 2026 snapshot)
Premium Pass $8.99 (30 tiers) · Dungeon Pack $1.99 · Slime Club Membership $9.99 (sweep tickets,
quick mining, daily perks) · Auto Blessing $4.99 · Remove ad $29.99 · Tier10 $9.99 · Special Package
Tier 10 $9.99 · Special Package Tier 20 $19.99 · Slime Promotion Package $9.99 · Slime Limit
Package $24.99. Currency packs exist (a $15 and a $100 Diamond pack are cited) but are not in the
visible list: **unknown** why.

Player tier list (Dec 2024, opinion): No Ads > Premium Pass > early loot/alchemy packs > memberships
> cosmetic/promo packs > plain currency packs.

## Pressure tactics reported (which we will NOT copy)
- Purchase pop-ups on app open and on the losing screen.
- Top-tier heroes only via payment (later softened by the 2026 Slime Summon).
- Multiple premium currencies and two memberships plus three pass tracks at once.

## Our honest equivalents
| Theirs | Ours (policy-compliant) |
|---|---|
| Remove Ads $29.99 | One **Ad-free** purchase, modest price, shown exactly |
| Premium Pass 30 tiers | Season pass premium track (exists), 30 tiers, free track always meaningful |
| Club membership (sweeps, quick mining) | **VIP**: Camp sweep tickets, 4× speed, extra offline hours; no exclusive power |
| 20-min ad buffs that escalate | **Hype Week** buffs: 20-min cash/power boosts via rewarded ad *or* a small cash cost, capped per day, no escalation beyond 2 levels |
| Free ad summons that grow | 1 free ad recruit per banner per day; the ad never changes the odds |
| Flying chests, roulette | **Fan gifts** (tap to claim, ad optional for ×2), **Halftime spin** 1 free/day |
| Dynamic "out of currency" offers | Never. Show the free way to earn instead |
| Purchase pop-ups on open/loss | Never |
