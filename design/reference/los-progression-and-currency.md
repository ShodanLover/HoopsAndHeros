# Legend of Slime: progression and currency (public-source reference)

*Compiled 2026-11-10. Paraphrased from public sources (listed at the bottom); nothing from the
game's files. Numbers are as reported by guides in 2023–2026 and may be outdated. **unknown** marks
what could not be confirmed.*

Guide consensus on priority: ATK → crit chance → crit damage → ASPD/multishot; HP only when a boss
demands it. Early: gear summons first, then skills, then companions; later, companions.

## The progressive gacha (the key retention mechanic)

- Each banner (gear, skills, companions) has a **level 1–10** with an XP bar; every pull adds XP
  equal to the number of pulls. Levelling the banner **improves the drop table**.
- Reported tables: L1 100% common. L2 75% common / 25% uncommon. L10 ≈ 9.21% common, 9.5%
  uncommon, 70% epic (remainder legendary and above, **exact split unknown**). Legendary appears
  from L6. Odds are shown in-game behind an "i" button.
- Free pulls: 3 per banner per day by ad; the size of an ad pull grows with use.
- Duplicates always matter: they enhance the item. At level 100, every 5 further copies convert into
  the next item (developer FAQ).
- 2026 Slime Summon (heroes): SS-grade guaranteed within 100 pulls (counter resets on SS); S-grade
  or better every 10; a 3-slot wish list gives chosen SS slimes about +50% weight; 1 Golden Jelly =
  200 Diamonds or stage/expedition rewards.

Design reading: the player is rewarded for *volume* of pulls twice over (banner level and duplicate
enhancement), so there is never a bad pull and never a reason to hoard.

## Pacing facts we could confirm
- Mines unlock at 3-10; relics at 7-5; slime levelling needs mine depth 250 m.
- Boss fights are timed; failure is non-punitive (farm the previous stage).
- AFK income follows the last cleared stage, capped at 12 h.
- Dungeon tiers unlock sequentially; difficulty arrows let you drop down.
- Time-to-wall, stage count and enemy HP curve: **unknown** from public sources.

## What to copy in spirit, with our numbers
1. A flat, always-affordable stat ladder next to the fight (our Team Growth panel).
2. Banners whose odds improve with pulls, with the odds table one tap away (fits our full-odds rule).
3. Owned effects on everything collectible, so duplicates and "bad" pulls still add power.
4. Several small materials each tied to one activity (keys→dungeons, ore→research), so each
   faucet has a reason to exist. We should keep ours to 3–4 materials, not 10.
5. Non-punitive walls and offline income tied to best stage.
