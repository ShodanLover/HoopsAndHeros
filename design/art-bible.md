# Art Bible: Basketball Empire (16-bit)

*Owned by the art-director agent. The visual source of truth.*

## Direction
SNES-era arcade sports: bold silhouettes, a limited palette, dithered gradients, chunky UI windows,
pixel fonts. It should read instantly on a phone in portrait.

## Resolution and scale
- Canvases (court, contests) render at about **170 px** internal width, upscaled by an integer factor
  (`BE.pixel.pixFor`) with `image-rendering: pixelated`. No sub-pixel movement for UI or shake.
- After drawing, the frame is snapped to **4 bits per channel** with a **Bayer 4×4** ordered dither
  (`BE.pixel.quantize`).
- Court player sprites: **24×35 px** canvas (about 26 px tall), 1px dark outline (`#0c0a18`), 2 tones per material
  (base and shade). Source: `tools/sprites/player_spec.py` → codeart2d → `js/sprite-data.js`.
- Portraits: **40×40** sprites with a 1px outline (generated by `js/pixel.js`, to be upgraded with the
  sprite-forge hibit finish).
- UI and gear icons: **16×16**, two tones (full and translucent `currentColor`), shown only at 16 or 32 px.
  Source: `tools/sprites/icon_spec.py` → codeart2d → `js/icon-data.js`. Effects: 8–32 px, 4–8 frames.

## Palette and color language
- UI base: deep navy panels (`--bg`, `--panel`), off-white text, basketball orange `--accent` for primary
  actions, gold `--gold` for premium and championship moments.
- Green = cash and gains, red = loss and danger, purple = gems.
- Rarity colors: Common `#a9b4c2`, Uncommon `#38d996`, Rare `#3aa8ff`, Epic `#b46bff`, Legendary `#ffb22e`,
  Mythic `#ff3f7a`. Use them only for rarity.
- Each world has its own arena palette (`worlds[].theme` in `js/data.js`): asphalt and sunset for
  streetball, varnished wood for the high school gym, dark arenas with spotlights for college and the pros,
  flags for international, gold for the global stage, neon for Legends, marble for the Hall of Fame, and
  a cosmic look for the Multiverse.

## Type
- **Press Start 2P**: short titles, scores, big numbers only (it's wide, so keep it small: 12–20px in the UI).
- **Pixelify Sans**: body text, labels and buttons (13px minimum).

## UI windows
- Notched pixel corners (`clip-path`), not rounded corners. A double inset border (light inner, dark
  outer) in the style of RPG menus.
- Buttons: a solid fill with a 3px top highlight and a 4px bottom shadow; pressing moves them down 2px.
- Gradients are stepped (hard color stops) or dithered, never smooth.
- Rarity shimmer and blinking use `steps()` timing.

## Animation
- Players: idle 2 frames, run 4, dribble 2, shoot 3, jump/dunk 3, block 2, celebrate 4 (style per
  athlete). 8–12 fps.
- Big moments escalate: screen shake (integer px), a flash, confetti, a crowd bounce and a banner.

## Originality and sourcing
Every character, team, logo and arena is original. Don't trace or copy existing games, leagues, team
marks or real players. Outside art needs a compatible license and an entry in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
The pixel-art-atlas dataset is a reference for style and palette only until its license is confirmed.
